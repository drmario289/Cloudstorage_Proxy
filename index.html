Security Notice: SIM-Swapping Threat Disclosure

Recent security analyses demonstrate that traditional SMS-based one-time passwords are vulnerable to SIM-swapping attacks. Attackers can socially engineer mobile network operators to port your phone number to an attacker-controlled SIM card, entirely intercepting your SMS verification codes without your physical device.

To ensure absolute protection against network interception, security standards recommend using more resilient authenticators such as Passkeys. Such other methods cryptographically bind your credentials to your physical device, neutralizing SIM-swapping risks entirely.
